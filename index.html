Arena Elements

Furniture and passive buffs to set up in the arena before you summon.

Core three: Campfire + Heart Lantern + Bast Statue. The Campfire and Heart Lantern add about +1.5 HP/s regen and multiply healing by 1.1×, and the Bast Statue adds +5 defense.
Heart statues: place as many as you can and wire them to a 1/4 or 1/8 second timer.
